The ledger sheet

Where it stands now, after six turns (newest at the top): the palette is corrected (the action colour is separated from “verified”, chain blue is retired, and contrast is raised — 2); the type scale is rebuilt and the screens snapped onto it (3, 6); a custom icon set shipped as an icon font (3b, 4); Anek + Plex Mono replace the system font (5); and the logo is revised to 6a. The four cards below are the turn-1 principles, and they still hold.

01 · CHROME
One bar, one pip
Title + subtitle + three unlabelled icons become a monogram, a seat name and a live block pip — which is also the way into the ledger. Every seat gets the same bottom nav; the pharmacy is no longer the only one.
02 · RECEIPTS
On-chain objects look on-chain
Anything whose truth is a write gets a receipt card with a BLK · tx footer in mono. (It started with a blue rule; turn 2 retired the blue.) Off-chain rows stay plain. The claim becomes visible rather than asserted.
03 · COUNTABLE
A tick per carton
The 4%-verified progress bar becomes 25 ticks — one per carton, the misroute red in its own position. Credit becomes 8 arcs, one per cycle. Proportions you can count beat proportions you estimate.
04 · DENSITY
One step back down
A 24px screen title and a 19px card head left three facts on a 844px phone. Titles 20, figures 22–26, body 14. Field keeps 44px targets; the desk portals get a table, not a stack of slabs.
6Audit: does the redesign obey its own rules?

I counted every font: declaration in this file with a script. Short answer: no. My own mockups have the same drift I flagged in the Flutter code, and my recommended logo, 2a, breaks three logo principles. All ten phone screens are now set in the 5a fonts and snapped onto the scale and tracking rule below, so what you see is what would ship.

TEXT · FAIL
Was 19 sizes; now snapped onto the scale
I drew turn 1 before the 3a scale existed. Before: 19 sizes, and 88 runs at 9–10.5px, under my own 11px floor. After snapping every phone screen: 10 sizes remain, all on the scale (sans 12.5 / 14 / 15 / 16 / 20, mono 11 / 12.5 / 15 / 22 / 28), and nothing is under 11px. TEXT · FAIL
Was 12 letter-spacings; now 3
The phones used −.03 to +.16em in twelve steps too close to tell apart. They're now snapped to the rule: −0.02em at 20px and up, 0 for 12.5–16px, and +0.12em for uppercase labels only.
TEXT · GAP IN THE SCALE
There's no size for an amount in a list row
Mono sizes jump from 12.5 (data) to 22 (figure), which is why I kept inventing 15, 17, 18 and 19. That drift shows a step is missing: add amount · 15 / 600 mono. That makes 10 tokens.
TEXT · PASS
Each face kept to its own job
Across all 8 screens, mono only carries ids, hashes, amounts, blocks and labels. Names and sentences are always in sans. The 7 uses of weight 700 are ✓ and ✕ glyphs, not text, and they'll go away once those become LampIcons.

5The typeface — the system cascade is already broken here
THE ARGUMENT THAT FAILS
One line on your busiest screen is set in two unrelated faces
typography.dart drops the bundled sans so text “matches the operating system it is sitting in”. That reasoning is sound for lu.ma, an English-language web product. It does not survive contact with this app: sansCascade ends in NotoSansBengali, so every Bengali glyph resolves to a bundled face while the Latin beside it resolves to the system face — on the same line, in the same sentence, on the launcher and on every MPO route card.

MARK · CUSTOMPAINTER
Three rounded rects, not a PNG
lamp_mark.dart currently does Image.asset on a raster — which is exactly why the mark cannot re-colour for dark mode and why it sits as a green smudge on the dark launcher. The new mark is three RRects, so it takes a colour, scales to any size, and needs no asset at all.
LampMark(size: 28, color: c.brand) // canvas.drawRRect × 3 // two strokes + one fill
Keeps the same public API — size, markOnly — so no call site changes. Launcher icons stay PNG exports of the same painter.
